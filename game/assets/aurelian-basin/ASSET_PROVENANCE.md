## Current candidate status

The first engine-native composition candidate contains no imported third-party model binaries yet. It uses only Godot procedural primitive meshes to prove the fixed camera, continuous basin composition, seven parcel hit areas and state-backed claim flow before importing a curated KayKit subset.

This does not authorize any other asset pack. KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack remains the only permitted source for imported production models in Sprint 2. A later commit importing the curated subset must record the exact upstream commit/archive and SHA-256 for every copied binary or source archive before visual acceptance.

## Prohibited

- paid Extra or Source tiers without explicit approval;
- mirrors or repackaged downloads;
- legacy Pixel Nations SVG/CSS art;
- Kenney, generated models or another KayKit pack;
- unverified binary files.
